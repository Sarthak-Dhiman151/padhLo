## Indian Research Repositories

### INFLIBNET Repositories

- [Shodhganga](https://shodhganga.inflibnet.ac.in/) [Official] [Govt] [Open Access] [PDF] - 500,000 Indian doctoral theses.
- [ShodhGangotri](https://shodhgangotri.inflibnet.ac.in/) [Official] [Govt] [Open Access] - Approved Ph.D. synopses.
- [e-ShodhSindhu](https://ess.inflibnet.ac.in/) [Official] [Govt] - Journal consortium for universities.
- [Vidwan Database](https://vidwan.inflibnet.ac.in/) [Official] [Govt] - Expert database of researchers.
- [IRINS](https://irins.inflibnet.ac.in/) [Official] [Govt] - Faculty citation metrics network.

### Council Journals & Publications

- [CSIR-NIScPR Open Journals](https://nopr.niscpr.res.in/) [Official] [Govt] [Open Access] - Indian science journals.
- [Indian Academy of Sciences](https://www.ias.ac.in/Journals/Overview/) [Open Access] [Free] - Physics and biology journals.
- [ICSSR Portal](https://icssr.org/) [Official] [Govt] [Scheme] - Social science grants.

## Global Open Access & Preprints

### Preprint Servers

- [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/) [Open Access] [Free] - Physics, maths, and CS preprints.
- [bioRxiv](https://www.biorxiv.org/) [Open Access] [Free] - Biology preprints.
- [medRxiv](https://www.medrxiv.org/) [Open Access] [Free] - Health sciences preprints.
- [SSRN](https://www.ssrn.com/) [Open Access] [Free] - Economics, law, social science.
- [OSF Preprints](https://osf.io/preprints/) [Open Access] [Free] - Multi-disciplinary preprint search.

### Search Engines & Mapping

- [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/) [Free] [Tool] - Global academic search.
- [Semantic Scholar](https://www.semanticscholar.org/) [Free] [Tool] - AI paper discovery.
- [DOAJ](https://doaj.org/) [Open Access] [Free] - Verified open-access journals.
- [CORE](https://core.ac.uk/) [Open Access] [Free] - Worldwide repository aggregator.
- [The Lens](https://www.lens.org/) [Open Access] [Free] - Patents linked to scholarly literature.
- [Connected Papers](https://www.connectedpapers.com/) [Tool] [Free] - Visual related-paper graphs.
- [ResearchRabbit](https://www.researchrabbit.ai/) [Tool] [Free] - Citation maps and alerts.

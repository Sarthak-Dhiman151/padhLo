## Indian National AI Initiatives

### IndiaAI & Bhashini

- [IndiaAI Mission Portal](https://indiaai.gov.in/) [Official] [Govt] - National AI portal and policies.
- [Bhashini Translation Mission](https://bhashini.gov.in/) [Official] [Govt] [Open Access] - Indian language AI models.
- [Skill India AI Courses](https://www.skillindiadigital.gov.in/) [Official] [Govt] [Course] [Free] - AI and digital literacy.

### Research Centers of Excellence

- [RBCDSAI (IIT Madras)](https://rbcdsai.iitm.ac.in/) [Official] [Open Access] - Data science research publications.
- [KCIS (IIIT Hyderabad)](https://kcis.iiit.ac.in/) [Official] - Vision, speech, and robotics lab.
- [AI4Bharat (IIT Madras)](https://ai4bharat.iitm.ac.in/) [Official] [Open Access] - Indic language models.

## Open-Source LLMs & Local Execution

### Model Registries

- [Hugging Face Hub](https://huggingface.co/models) [Open Access] [Repo] - 1M+ open-weight models.
- [ModelScope](https://www.modelscope.cn/) [Open Access] [Repo] - Multilingual model registry.

### Local & Private Runtimes

- [Ollama](https://ollama.com/) [Tool] [Free] [Open Access] - Run Llama and Mistral locally.
- [LM Studio](https://lmstudio.ai/) [Tool] [Free] - Desktop app for offline GGUF models.
- [llama.cpp](https://github.com/ggerganov/llama.cpp) [Repo] [Free] [Open Access] - CPU/GPU inference engine.

### Frameworks

- [LangChain](https://www.langchain.com/) [Docs] [Open Access] [Free] - LLM application framework.
- [LlamaIndex](https://www.llamaindex.ai/) [Docs] [Open Access] [Free] - Private document querying.

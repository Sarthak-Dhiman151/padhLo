## How to use this wiki

- **Search** — Press `/` or `Ctrl + K` (`Cmd + K`) anywhere to open the search box. It matches topic names, section headings and keywords; Enter opens the first result.
- **Sidebar** — The left column lists every page in the wiki, grouped into Getting started, Student resources and Project. The page you are reading stays highlighted.
- **On this page** — On wide screens the right column mirrors the current page's headings, scrolls to the one you click and tracks the section in view.
- **Section markers** — A filled triangle marks a page title, an outline triangle marks a section or sub-section. Every marker is a stable anchor you can link to.
- **Link tags** — Each entry follows the same shape: the name, a link, then a few words naming what the page contains. Tags describe the resource, not the subject.
- **External links** — Links to portals, archives and courses open in a new tab. Nothing is hosted here.

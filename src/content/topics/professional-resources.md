## Internships & Fellowships

### Government & Public Policy

- [NITI Aayog Internships](https://www.niti.gov.in/internship) [Official] [Govt] [Scheme] - Policy research internships.
- [RBI Summer Internship](https://opportunities.rbi.org.in/) [Official] [Govt] [Stipend] - Economics and finance placements.
- [MEA Internships](https://www.mea.gov.in/internship.htm) [Official] [Govt] - Foreign affairs research.
- [CCI Internship](https://www.cci.gov.in/internship) [Official] [Govt] - Competition law and economics.

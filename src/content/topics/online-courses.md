## Indian National MOOC Platforms

### SWAYAM

- [SWAYAM Official Portal](https://swayam.gov.in/) [Official] [Govt] [Course] [Free] - School to postgraduate courses.
- [SWAYAM Credit Regulations](https://www.ugc.gov.in/pdfnews/9331821_SWAYAM-Regulations-2021.pdf) [Official] [Govt] [PDF] - UGC credit transfer rules.
- [National Coordinators Directory](https://swayam.gov.in/nc_details/) [Official] [Govt] - NPTEL, UGC, IIMB, CEC.

### NPTEL

- [NPTEL Official Portal](https://nptel.ac.in/) [Official] [Course] [Free] - IIT and IISc online courses.
- [NPTEL Stars & Domains](https://nptel.ac.in/nptelStars/) [Official] [Course] - Domain certifications.
- [NPTEL Video Archive](https://www.youtube.com/@nptelhrd) [Official] [Video] [Free] - Recorded classroom lectures.

### Spoken Tutorial (IIT Bombay)

- [Spoken Tutorial Portal](https://spoken-tutorial.org/) [Official] [Free] [Course] - Open-source software tutorials.
- [Spoken Tutorial Tests](https://spoken-tutorial.org/participant/login/) [Official] [Free] - Certification test portal.

### Open Distance Learning

- [eGyanKosh (IGNOU)](https://egyankosh.ac.in/) [Official] [Govt] [Open Access] - Digital self-learning repository.
- [CEC Portal](https://cec.nic.in/) [Official] [Govt] [Video] [Free] - Undergraduate educational media.

## Premier University OpenCourseWare

### MIT OpenCourseWare

- [MIT OpenCourseWare](https://ocw.mit.edu/) [Open Access] [Course] [Free] - 2,500+ MIT courses.
- [MIT OCW YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@mitocw) [Video] [Free] - Recorded university lectures.
- [MIT OCW Educator Resources](https://ocw.mit.edu/pages/for-educators/) [Open Access] [Notes] [Free] - Syllabi and solution keys.

### Global Elite Universities

- [Harvard Online Courses](https://pll.harvard.edu/catalog/free) [Course] [Free] - University open catalog.
- [CS50x: Computer Science](https://cs50.harvard.edu/x/) [Course] [Free] - Foundational CS with problem sets.
- [Stanford Online Courses](https://online.stanford.edu/free-courses) [Course] [Free] - Engineering and medicine.
- [Stanford Engineering Everywhere](https://see.stanford.edu/) [Course] [Free] - CS106A/B and linear systems.
- [OpenLearn (Open University)](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/) [Course] [Free] - Short courses with statements.

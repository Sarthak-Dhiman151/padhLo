## Indian Digital Libraries & Archives

### National Digital Library of India

- [National Digital Library of India](https://ndl.iitkgp.ac.in/) [Official] [Govt] [Open Access] - 90 million educational resources.
- [NDLI School Window](https://ndl.iitkgp.ac.in/) [Official] [Govt] - Board-aligned study collections.
- [NDLI Institutional Access](https://ndl.iitkgp.ac.in/homestudy/institutional) [Official] [Govt] - Full-text borrowing.

### School & Postgraduate Government Books

- [NCERT Textbooks Portal](https://ncert.nic.in/textbook.php) [Official] [Govt] [Textbook] [PDF] [Free] - School textbooks.
- [e-Adhyayan (e-PG Pathshala)](https://epgp.inflibnet.ac.in/) [Official] [Govt] [Textbook] [Open Access] - Postgraduate e-books.
- [National Book Trust](https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/) [Official] [Govt] [Textbook] - Indian literature and biographies.
- [Sahitya Akademi](https://sahitya-akademi.gov.in/) [Official] [Govt] - Publications in 24 languages.

### Historical Archives

- [Rare Book Society of India](https://www.rarebooksocietyofindia.org/) [Open Access] - Rare Indian historical books.
- [Abhilekh Patal](https://www.abhilekh-patal.in/) [Official] [Govt] [Open Access] - National Archives documents.

## E-Reader Software & Tools

### Desktop Readers

- [Calibre](https://calibre-ebook.com/) [Tool] [Free] [Open Access] - Ebook manager and converter.
- [Sumatra PDF](https://www.sumatrapdfreader.org/) [Tool] [Free] - Lightweight PDF and EPUB viewer.
- [Foliate](https://johnfactotum.github.io/foliate/) [Tool] [Free] [Open Access] - GTK ebook reader for Linux.

### Mobile E-Readers

- [ReadEra (Android)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.readera) [App] [Free] - Ad-free offline book reader.
- [Moon+ Reader (Android)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.flyersoft.moonreader) [App] [Free] - Customizable mobile reader.
- [Apple Books (iOS)](https://www.apple.com/apple-books/) [App] - Built-in reader for iPhone and iPad.

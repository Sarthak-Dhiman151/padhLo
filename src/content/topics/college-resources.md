## Higher Education Regulatory Bodies

### UGC & AICTE Portals

- [University Grants Commission](https://www.ugc.gov.in/) [Official] [Govt] - Approved university lists and regulations.
- [UGC Distance Education Bureau](https://deb.ugc.ac.in/) [Official] [Govt] - Verify approved distance programs.
- [All India Council for Technical Education](https://www.aicte-india.org/) [Official] [Govt] - Technical education regulator.
- [AICTE Process Handbook](https://www.aicte-india.org/bulletins/approval-process-handbook) [Official] [Govt] [PDF] - Institutional standards.

### Accreditation & Rankings

- [NAAC Portal](http://naac.gov.in/) [Official] [Govt] - Institutional assessment grades.
- [National Board of Accreditation](https://www.nbaind.org/) [Official] [Govt] - Program accreditation.
- [NIRF Rankings](https://www.nirfindia.org/) [Official] [Govt] - Ministry of Education rankings.
